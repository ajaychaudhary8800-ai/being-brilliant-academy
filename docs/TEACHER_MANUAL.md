# Teacher manual

Sign in at `/login` and open `/teacher`. The portal shows only assigned classes and students. Do not attempt to access another teacher's batch or reuse another person's session.

Use **My Classes** and **Timetable** to confirm the day's schedule. Mark attendance during the class and review the batch and date before submitting. Create homework with a realistic due date, supported attachment type, and marks ceiling. Evaluate submissions with marks no greater than the maximum and include actionable feedback.

Enter examination marks only for assigned subjects and batches. Review totals before publishing results. Use student performance insights to identify weak topics; do not treat automated recommendations as final academic decisions.

Upload only institution-owned or licensed notes and recordings. Remove personal information from teaching attachments. Use announcements for class-wide information and messaging for individual communication. Report incorrect assignments, access, or student data to an administrator immediately.
