# Data Exit & Retention Policy

## 1. During subscription

Client may use available product exports/reports according to its plan and permissions. Custom exports, historical transformations or migration assistance may be separately scoped.

## 2. Exit window

Unless an Order Form states otherwise, following subscription expiry/termination Client has a **30-day exit window** to request or complete a reasonable export of then-available Client Data, subject to payment of undisputed overdue charges and lawful restrictions.

Provider may provide standard exports through available product functions. Bespoke migration to another vendor, custom schemas or engineering support is separately chargeable.

## 3. Deletion timeline

Subject to legal/security holds and ordinary backup rotation, Provider will target deletion or irreversible de-association of active-system Client Data within **60 days after the exit window closes**.

Provider may retain:
- invoices/tax records and contractual records required by law;
- audit/security records reasonably necessary for fraud, incident and dispute purposes;
- records subject to a lawful preservation request;
- residual backup copies until they age out under ordinary backup rotation.

Retained data remains protected and is not restored into active service except for legitimate recovery, security or legal purposes.

## 4. Client responsibilities

Before termination, Client should identify legally required institutional records and export them in time. Provider is not the Client's statutory records archive unless an Order Form expressly says so.

Client is responsible for secure storage and access control after export.

## 5. Account closure

After the exit window, Provider may disable tenant logins and reusable custom-domain routing. Domain registration remains the domain owner's responsibility.

## 6. Data migration assistance

Migration services beyond standard export are governed by a scoped SOW/Change Request and the commercial catalogue.
