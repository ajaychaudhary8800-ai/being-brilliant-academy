## Precedence

Unless a signed document expressly states otherwise:

1. signed Order Form / Quotation;
2. signed Implementation SOW or Change Order;
3. SaaS Subscription Agreement;
4. Data Processing Addendum;
5. Service Level Agreement;
6. published/attached product documentation.

A signed document may override a lower-precedence document only for the specific subject it addresses.

## Commercial source of truth

Commercial pricing, plan limits, implementation charges, add-ons and discount guardrails are controlled by `docs/COMMERCIAL_PACKAGING_PRICING.md`. Do not type ad-hoc commercial promises into proposals that conflict with that document.

## Legal/compliance posture

These templates are operational business templates, not a substitute for client-specific legal advice. Before the first external execution, Indian counsel should review:

- Provider legal identity and registered-office details.
- Arbitration/jurisdiction wording.
- Limitation-of-liability caps.
- Indemnity scope.
- Data retention and deletion commitments.
- Subprocessor list and international transfers.
- Child-data workflows.
- Any school-board/state-specific requirements.

As of 25 September 2026, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 are under phased commencement. The templates require compliance with applicable law when each provision becomes effective, so the contract does not need to be rewritten solely because a scheduled DPDP provision commences.

## Execution controls

- Never execute a proposal without a valid quotation/order form.
- Never enable Enterprise-only modules by side agreement without a matching commercial plan or documented platform override.
- Never promise unlimited branches, users or students unless a dedicated plan is created.
- Never promise data migration, integrations, on-site training or custom reports unless explicitly scoped and priced.
- Every client must have a named authorized administrator and billing contact.
- Every production go-live requires UAT/go-live sign-off or a written risk acceptance approved by Provider management.
