# Sales, Legal & Client Documentation Pack

This folder is the controlled commercial contracting pack for **Being Brilliant ERP + LMS + CRM**, supplied by **Adhyay Eduventure Private Limited** ("Provider").

## ERP access

Platform Super Admin users can access the complete controlled pack from **Sales Documents / Legal Pack** at `/admin/legal-sales`. The workspace supports source viewing, placeholder-based client working-copy generation, Markdown download and print/PDF. Generated copies never overwrite these controlled source files.

## Provider execution particulars

Management-confirmed provider particulars are recorded in `EXECUTION_PARTICULARS.md`.

Before first external signature or public legal publication:
- cross-check the registered office, CIN, GSTIN and authorized signatory against current official/company records;
- activate and test `legal@beingbrilliantedu.com`, `privacy@beingbrilliantedu.com` and `support@beingbrilliantedu.com`;
- confirm any client-facing support phone from approved business records before publication;
- insert bank/payment details only from approved company finance records into the specific issued quotation/invoice; and
- complete Indian counsel review of the controlled legal pack.

Do not copy an address, GSTIN, contact or bank detail from an unverified directory.

## Contract hierarchy

Unless an executed Order Form states otherwise, precedence should be:
1. signed Order Form / negotiated special terms;
2. signed DPA for personal-data matters;
3. signed Implementation SOW for implementation scope;
4. Master SaaS Agreement;
5. SLA/Support Policy;
6. Acceptable Use Policy;
7. Data Exit & Retention Policy.

The commercial prices and capacity catalogue are controlled by `../COMMERCIAL_PACKAGING_PRICING.md`.

## Legal-status note

These documents are operational contract templates and must receive Indian counsel review before the first external signature and whenever law, hosting, security architecture, payment flows or material product behavior changes.

The DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 have phased commencement. The templates are drafted to be DPDP-ready without claiming that provisions scheduled for future commencement are already in force.

## Infrastructure truth rule

Never promise a control that has not been implemented and verified. Step 5 off-site backup and isolated restore evidence is complete; however, no fixed contractual RPO/RTO, cross-region restoration guarantee or DR service credit may be added unless it is separately approved and supported by the contracted production design.
