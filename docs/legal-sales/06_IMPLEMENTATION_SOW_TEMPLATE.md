- on-site work;

## 7. Change control

A request is a Change Request where it materially changes scope, capacity, data migration, integrations, deliverables, timeline or assumptions.

Provider will document impact on fees/timeline. Work begins after written approval where additional charges apply.

## 8. Acceptance

A deliverable is accepted when:

- Client signs acceptance; or
- Client uses it in production; or
- Client does not provide specific material rejection reasons within [5] business days after delivery.

This deemed-acceptance clause should be reviewed by counsel before first external use.

## 9. UAT severity

- Blocker: prevents contracted production go-live and no reasonable workaround.
- Major: material contracted workflow defect with significant impact.
- Minor: non-blocking issue/cosmetic/request.

Go-live requires zero unresolved Blockers unless Client signs written risk acceptance.
