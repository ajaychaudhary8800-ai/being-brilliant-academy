# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This DPA forms part of the SaaS Agreement/Order Form between the Client and Adhyay Eduventure Private Limited ("Provider").

## 1. Roles

For personal data in student, parent/guardian, teacher, employee and institutional records that Client submits and for which Client determines purposes and essential means, the parties intend:
- Client to act as the relevant Data Fiduciary/controller (or equivalent role); and
- Provider to act as Data Processor/processor on Client's documented instructions,
to the extent those roles apply under applicable law.

Provider may act independently as Data Fiduciary/controller for Provider's own business-contact, account-security, fraud-prevention, billing, legal-compliance and service-administration data.

## 2. Processing details

**Subject matter:** provision, security, support and administration of the contracted education SaaS.  
**Duration:** subscription term plus permitted retention/deletion period.  
**Data subjects:** students, children, parents/guardians, applicants, teachers, employees, contractors and authorized users as configured by Client.  
**Data categories:** identifiers, contact/account data, academic records, attendance, fee/payment metadata, communications, employment/admin records, uploaded documents and other fields Client chooses to use.  
**Purposes:** only contracted functionality, support, security, backup, authorized integration and legal compliance.

Client must not upload categories it is not authorized to process or that are unnecessary for the contracted purpose.

## 3. Documented instructions

Provider will process Client Personal Data only on documented Client instructions embodied in the contract, product configuration and lawful support requests, unless law requires otherwise. If Provider believes an instruction violates applicable data-protection law, it may pause the affected instruction and notify Client where permitted.

## 4. Client obligations

Client is responsible for:
- lawful collection and disclosure to Provider;
- required notices and consent/authorization;
- data minimization and accuracy;
- responding as Data Fiduciary/controller to Data Principal/data-subject requests;
- determining retention required for educational, employment, tax or other legal records.

## 5. Children

Where child/student data is processed, Client is responsible for the legal basis and for verifiable parental/guardian consent where required by applicable law. Provider will not use Client child data for targeted advertising or independent behavioural profiling.

## 6. Confidentiality

Provider will limit access to personnel/contractors who require it for their role and who are subject to confidentiality obligations.

## 7. Security safeguards

Provider will maintain safeguards described in the Security Schedule and appropriate to the risk and current architecture. This includes tenant access controls, authentication/authorization controls, secure transport for production access, logging/monitoring and controlled release processes.

No certification (including ISO 27001 or SOC 2) is represented unless explicitly stated in an executed Order Form.

## 8. Security incidents

Provider will notify Client **without undue delay and target notification within 24 hours after confirming** a security incident that results in unauthorized access to, disclosure, alteration or loss of Client Personal Data.

Initial notice may be preliminary. Provider will provide information reasonably available about nature, affected data, likely impact, containment and remediation, and will update material facts as investigation proceeds.

Client remains responsible for its regulatory/Data Principal notifications unless law directly imposes a separate duty on Provider.

## 9. Requests and complaints

Taking into account the nature of processing, Provider will provide reasonable assistance through available product/admin functions for Client to respond to lawful access, correction, erasure or grievance requests. Bespoke extraction or engineering may be chargeable if outside standard functionality.

## 10. Subprocessors

Client gives general authorization for Provider to use subprocessors reasonably necessary for hosting, email, payment, monitoring, storage, messaging and support. Provider remains responsible for imposing appropriate data-protection/confidentiality obligations on subprocessors for the processing they perform.

Material subprocessor/hosting changes that materially affect Client Personal Data will be notified where contractually or legally required.

## 11. Cross-border/hosting

Primary hosting location and any residency commitment are only those expressly stated in the Order Form. Provider will comply with applicable restrictions on transfers as they become effective. Client must not assume India-only data residency unless the Order Form expressly says so.

## 12. Retention, return and deletion

On termination, export and deletion follow the Data Exit & Retention Policy. Provider may retain data where required by law, for security/audit evidence, dispute preservation or ordinary backup rotation, with access restricted to the retained purpose.

## 13. Audit information

On reasonable written request, Provider will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate contractual processing controls, subject to confidentiality, security and third-party restrictions. On-site audits are not included by default and may require scope, timing and cost agreement.

## 14. DPDP transition

The parties acknowledge phased commencement of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. Contract controls intended to support future-effective requirements should be operationalized by their applicable commencement dates.

## 15. Precedence

For a conflict concerning processing of Client Personal Data, this DPA controls over the Master Agreement, except a signed Order Form clause that expressly identifies and overrides the relevant DPA provision.
