# SaaS Subscription Agreement

This SaaS Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on [Effective Date] between **Adhyay Eduventure Private Limited**, operating the Being Brilliant platform ("Provider"), and the entity identified in the applicable Order Form ("Client").

## 1. Contract structure

The Agreement includes this document, each signed Order Form, the SLA, DPA, Implementation SOW and signed Change Orders. Order Forms identify plan, capacity, term and fees.

## 2. Service

Provider grants Client a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the subscription term to access the contracted Being Brilliant SaaS modules for Client's internal educational and administrative operations.

No source code, ownership right or intellectual-property assignment is granted.

## 3. Authorized users and accounts

Client is responsible for:

- selecting authorized administrators;
- lawful creation of staff/student/parent/user accounts;
- protecting credentials;
- disabling users who no longer require access;
- ensuring users comply with acceptable-use and confidentiality obligations.

Client must promptly notify Provider of suspected unauthorized account use.

## 4. Client responsibilities

Client will:

- provide accurate configuration and migration data;
- maintain lawful authority for personal-data processing;
- provide legally required notices/consents to students, parents, employees and other data principals;
- obtain parental/guardian authorization where required for children;
- use the service only for lawful institutional purposes;
- not probe, attack, reverse engineer, scrape, bypass plan controls or interfere with platform security;
- not upload content it lacks rights to use.

## 5. Fees and taxes

Client will pay fees stated in the Order Form in advance unless otherwise agreed. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Third-party usage/provider charges are excluded unless the Order Form says otherwise.

Overdue subscriptions may enter past-due/billing-recovery status in accordance with the platform's billing lifecycle.

## 6. Term, renewal and cancellation

The initial term is stated in the Order Form. Renewal terms are as stated there.

Where cancellation-at-period-end is enabled, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period. Fees already paid are non-refundable except where required by law or expressly stated in the Order Form.

## 7. Plan entitlements and capacity

Client may use only contracted modules and capacity. Provider may technically enforce limits on branches, users, students and feature entitlements.

If usage exceeds a plan limit, Client must reduce usage, purchase capacity or upgrade. Provider will not be required to disable enforcement merely to preserve excess usage.

## 8. Changes and custom work

Bespoke development, integrations, custom reports, migrations and on-site services require a written SOW or Change Order. Oral requests do not alter scope.

## 9. Availability and support

Service levels and support commitments are defined in the SLA. Scheduled maintenance, force-majeure events, Client systems, third-party providers and unsupported configurations are treated as stated in the SLA.

## 10. Data protection and security

Personal-data processing is governed by the DPA. Provider will maintain reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service.

Client remains responsible for deciding why and how institutional personal data is collected and used, except where Provider processes data for its own legitimate billing, security, fraud-prevention, legal-compliance or account-management purposes.

## 11. Confidentiality

Each party must protect the other's non-public business, technical, financial and personal information using reasonable care and use it only for contract purposes.

Confidentiality does not cover information that is independently developed, lawfully public, lawfully received without restriction or required to be disclosed by law. Where legally permitted, the receiving party will give prior notice of compelled disclosure.

## 12. Intellectual property

Provider and its licensors retain all rights in the platform, software, documentation, workflows, designs, trademarks and improvements.

Client retains rights in Client Data and Client-provided branding/content. Client grants Provider the limited rights necessary to host, process, back up, transmit and display Client Data to provide the service.

## 13. Feedback

Client may provide suggestions. Provider may use non-confidential feedback without obligation, but will not publicly identify Client without permission.

## 14. Warranties

Each party warrants it has authority to enter this Agreement.

Provider warrants it will provide the service substantially in accordance with applicable documentation and use commercially reasonable skill and care.

Except as expressly stated and to the maximum extent permitted by law, the service is not warranted to be uninterrupted, error-free or suitable for every Client-specific statutory requirement.

## 15. Indemnity

Client will defend/indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Client-provided content, unlawful processing instructions, Client's violation of law, or Client's unauthorized use of third-party intellectual property.

Provider will defend/indemnify Client against a third-party claim that the unmodified contracted SaaS service infringes an Indian intellectual-property right, subject to prompt notice, control of defence and reasonable cooperation. Provider may modify/replace the affected component or terminate the affected service with a pro-rata refund of prepaid unused subscription fees.

## 16. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law:

- neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, special or punitive losses, lost profits or loss of goodwill, except where such exclusion is prohibited;
- each party's aggregate liability arising from the Agreement is capped at fees paid/payable by Client for the affected service during the **12 months preceding the event** giving rise to the claim.

The cap/exclusions do not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, unauthorized use of the other party's intellectual property, or liabilities that cannot legally be limited. Any different cap must be stated in the Order Form.

## 17. Suspension

Provider may suspend affected access where reasonably necessary for security, unlawful use, material breach, non-payment, platform integrity or legal requirements. Where practicable, Provider will give notice and limit suspension to the affected scope.

## 18. Termination

Either party may terminate for a material breach not cured within 30 days after written notice, except where immediate termination is reasonably required for illegality, fraud or severe security abuse.

On termination, access ceases at the applicable effective date. Data export/deletion is handled under the DPA and agreed offboarding process.

## 19. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond reasonable control, excluding payment obligations already due. The affected party will use reasonable efforts to mitigate impact.

## 20. Electronic contracting

The parties may execute and accept the Agreement/Order Forms electronically, subject to applicable law. Electronic records, authorized e-signature workflows and auditable electronic acceptance may be used for contract formation.

## 21. Notices

Contract notices must be sent to the legal/contact addresses stated in the Order Form. Operational support tickets are not formal legal notices unless expressly acknowledged as such.

## 22. Governing law and dispute resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.

Any dispute not resolved through good-faith senior-management discussions within 30 days may be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator mutually appointed under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Seat/venue: **Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh**, unless the Order Form states otherwise. Proceedings will be in English.

Courts having competent jurisdiction at Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh will have jurisdiction for interim relief and matters not capable of arbitration, subject to applicable law.

## 23. General

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except as part of a merger/reorganization or sale of substantially all relevant business/assets where the assignee assumes the obligations.

If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue. Waiver must be written. The Agreement is the complete agreement on its subject and supersedes prior inconsistent representations.
