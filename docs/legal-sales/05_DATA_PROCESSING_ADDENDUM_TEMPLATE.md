# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This Data Processing Addendum forms part of the SaaS Subscription Agreement between Adhyay Eduventure Private Limited ("Provider") and the Client.

## 1. Roles

For personal data that Client uploads or causes to be processed for institutional purposes ("Client Personal Data"):

- Client generally determines the purpose and means of processing and acts as the **Data Fiduciary/controller-equivalent** under applicable law.
- Provider acts as Client's **Data Processor/service provider** to the extent it processes Client Personal Data on Client's documented instructions.

Provider may separately act as a Data Fiduciary for limited Provider-controlled purposes such as account administration, billing, fraud/security monitoring, legal compliance and business-contact management.

## 2. Processing instructions

Provider will process Client Personal Data only:

- to provide, secure, support and improve the contracted service;
- to perform documented Client instructions consistent with the Agreement;
- as required by applicable law.

Provider will notify Client if an instruction appears unlawful, unless law prohibits notice.

## 3. Categories

Expected data may include:

- students and applicants;
- parents/guardians;
- teachers/employees/contractors;
- administrators and institutional contacts.

Data may include identity/contact information, academic records, attendance, fee/payment metadata, employment/HR data, communications, uploaded educational content and technical/security logs.

Client must not upload data unnecessary for the contracted purpose.

## 4. Children

Educational deployments may involve children. Client is responsible for establishing the lawful basis/authorization for processing children's data and for obtaining/verifying parental or lawful-guardian consent where required by applicable law.

Provider will reasonably support configured parental/guardian workflows within the service.

## 5. Security safeguards

Provider will maintain reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the service, including as applicable:

- logical access controls and role-based authorization;
- tenant isolation;
- secure authentication/session controls;
- encryption in transit;
- credential/secrets protection;
- logging/monitoring;
- backups and recovery controls;
- vulnerability/dependency management;
- least-privilege operational access;
- incident response procedures.

No security measure guarantees absolute prevention of all incidents.

## 6. Personnel confidentiality

Provider will restrict authorized personnel access to need-to-know purposes and require appropriate confidentiality obligations.

## 7. Subprocessors

Client authorizes Provider to use subprocessors reasonably necessary to deliver the service, including cloud hosting, email, payment, monitoring, storage and messaging providers.

Provider remains responsible for imposing data-protection/security obligations appropriate to the subprocessor's role.

Provider will maintain a current subprocessor list or provide it on reasonable request. Material new subprocessors affecting Client Personal Data may be notified through documented channels where required by contract/law.

## 8. Personal-data breach

Provider will notify Client **without undue delay** after confirming a breach of Client Personal Data for which Provider is the relevant processor, and will provide reasonably available information necessary for Client to assess legal notification duties.

Provider will reasonably cooperate with containment, investigation and remediation.

Client is responsible for notifications to affected Data Principals/regulators where Client is the Data Fiduciary, unless applicable law assigns a direct duty to Provider.

When the applicable DPDP breach-notification provisions are in force, the parties will cooperate so Client can meet required "without delay" notices and any applicable detailed reporting timeline.

## 9. Assistance with rights requests

Taking into account the nature of processing and available functionality, Provider will reasonably assist Client with requests concerning access, correction, erasure, grievance/complaint handling or other applicable data-principal rights.

Client remains responsible for validating the requester and deciding the lawful response.

## 10. Retention and deletion

During the subscription, Client controls ordinary record lifecycle using available product functions, subject to legal/technical constraints.

After termination, Provider will delete or return Client Personal Data according to the agreed offboarding process, except where retention is required by law, legitimate security/audit requirements, backup rotation or dispute preservation.

Where retained after termination, data remains protected and inaccessible for ordinary product use until deletion/expiry.

Specific export windows or guaranteed deletion deadlines must be stated in the Order Form/SOW if commercially required.

## 11. Data location and transfers

Primary hosting/data-location commitments, if required, must be stated in the Order Form. Provider will not make a representation of India-only hosting unless actually configured and contracted.

Cross-border processing/transfers will be handled in accordance with applicable Indian law and governmental restrictions in force at the relevant time.

## 12. Audit and compliance information

Provider will make reasonable compliance/security information available to Client, subject to confidentiality and security limitations.

On-site audits are not included in standard plans. Where legally required or Enterprise-contracted, audits must be coordinated to avoid compromising other tenants, security or operations and may be subject to reasonable cost recovery.

## 13. Client obligations

Client will:

- issue legally required notices;
- obtain legally required consent/authorization;
- avoid collecting excessive/unnecessary data;
- configure role access appropriately;
- promptly revoke departed users;
- maintain lawful retention rules;
- not instruct Provider to process data unlawfully.

## 14. DPDP transition

The parties acknowledge that the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 have phased commencement.

Each party will comply with provisions applicable to it as and when they are in force. Contract references to DPDP duties apply from the relevant statutory commencement date and do not imply that a not-yet-commenced duty is already legally operative.

## 15. Conflict

For data-protection matters, this DPA controls over inconsistent general terms, while a signed Order Form may impose stricter Client-specific data terms.
