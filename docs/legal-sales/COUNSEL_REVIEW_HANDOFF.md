## Counsel review questions

Counsel should review and confirm or amend the following before the pack is approved for external execution:

- Provider identity, execution block and authorized-signatory wording.
- Registered-office wording against current official MCA/company records.
- GST treatment and invoicing language.
- Governing law, New Delhi arbitration seat and court/jurisdiction language.
- Limitation of liability, exclusions, indemnities and payment/default remedies.
- Service-level wording, service credits, outage exclusions and support commitments.
- Customer data ownership, processor/fiduciary allocation and institution responsibilities.
- DPDP Act, 2023 readiness, including children's data and parental/guardian consent responsibilities.
- Security, subprocessors, cross-border processing, incident notification and deletion/exit wording.
- Privacy Notice and grievance-contact wording.
- Acceptable-use restrictions.
- Data-retention and termination/exit periods.
- Electronic execution, notices and enforceability of the commercial order-form structure.
- Any education-sector-specific obligations that should be added for schools, coaching institutes or minors.
