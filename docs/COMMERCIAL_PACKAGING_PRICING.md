# Commercial Packaging & Pricing

Effective for new India-based SaaS customers from 25 September 2026.

This is the approved launch commercial model for Being Brilliant ERP + LMS + CRM. It applies to schools, coaching centres, academies and training institutes unless a signed enterprise quote states otherwise.

Prices below are exclusive of applicable GST. The standard domestic India plans are configured at 18% GST. Export, SEZ and non-domestic treatment must be reviewed by finance before invoicing.

## 1. Standard plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Active students | Active users | Branches |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Essentials | ₹4,999 | ₹49,990 | 300 | 1,000 | 1 |
| Growth | ₹8,999 | ₹89,990 | 800 | 3,000 | 1 |
| Professional | ₹14,999 | ₹1,49,990 | 2,000 | 7,000 | 5 |
| Enterprise | ₹29,999 | ₹2,99,990 | 5,000 | 18,000 | 20 |

Annual billing is the default commercial recommendation. The annual amount equals ten monthly payments, effectively providing about two months free versus twelve monthly payments.

The limits are enforced against active production records. User capacity intentionally exceeds student capacity because student, parent, teacher, administrator and staff accounts all consume user capacity.

## 2. Feature packaging

### Essentials

For small institutions moving from spreadsheets/manual operations.

Included core platform:
- Student and admission records.
- Academic sessions, courses/programs, batches/sections and subjects.
- Timetable and attendance.
- Fee management, receipts, defaulters and core fee reports.
- Student, parent and teacher portals where applicable.
- Core institutional reports.
- One production branch/campus.
- Standard organization branding.

Not included:
- LMS.
- Admissions CRM.
- Communication module.
- Examination workflow.
- HR/payroll.
- Finance/accounting.
- Library.
- Inventory.
- Transport.
- Hostel.
- Advanced analytics.
- Multi-branch operations.
- White-label branding.
- Custom domain.

### Growth

For established single-campus schools/coaching institutes that need academic engagement and admissions automation.

Everything in Essentials plus:
- LMS and learning content.
- Admissions CRM and enquiry management.
- Communication, notices and announcements.
- Examination/test workflows.

Growth remains a single-branch plan.

### Professional

For larger institutions and multi-branch operators.

Everything in Growth plus:
- HR and payroll.
- Finance/accounting.
- Library.
- Inventory.
- Advanced analytics.
- Multi-branch operations, up to the contracted branch limit.

Professional does not include full white-label branding, a custom domain, hostel or transport modules.

### Enterprise

For large institutions, school groups and organizations requiring branded deployment.

Everything in Professional plus:
- Transport.
- Hostel.
- Full white-label branding.
- Custom tenant domain.
- Higher student/user/branch capacity.
- Enterprise implementation coordination.

A native white-label mobile app is not included unless separately developed and contracted.

## 3. Trial policy

- Essentials, Growth and Professional: 14-day SaaS trial.
- Enterprise: sales-led implementation; no automatic free trial.
- Trial data must not be represented as a production migration commitment.
- Large production imports, custom integrations and custom-domain activation begin only after commercial approval.
- Trial expiry follows the existing subscription lifecycle and billing-recovery controls.

## 4. Onboarding and implementation

For annual subscriptions:
- Standard remote tenant provisioning is included.
- Standard remote administrator onboarding/training is included.
- Basic configuration guidance is included.
- Legacy data migration is not automatically included.

For monthly subscriptions, a one-time implementation charge equal to one monthly subscription fee applies unless waived in a signed quote:
- Essentials: ₹4,999.
- Growth: ₹8,999.
- Professional: ₹14,999.
- Enterprise: ₹29,999.

## 5. Separately charged services

These are not part of the standard subscription unless explicitly written into the signed proposal:

- Legacy data migration: starting ₹15,000; complex migrations quoted after source-data review.
- Custom integrations/API work: starting ₹25,000 per scoped integration.
- Custom reports/workflows: quoted by scope.
- On-site training: ₹10,000 per trainer day plus approved travel/stay expenses.
- Dedicated/private infrastructure: Enterprise custom quote.
- SMS/WhatsApp/message-provider usage: charged separately according to the approved provider/credit arrangement.
- Payment-gateway charges: provider charges remain separate from the SaaS subscription.
- Domain registration/renewal: client responsibility unless separately contracted.
- Bespoke development: separate statement of work.

## 6. Capacity extensions

Do not manually bypass plan limits.

When a client needs additional capacity, create a contract-specific SaaS plan so the application and database continue enforcing the agreed limits.

Standard annual capacity references:
- Additional 250 active students plus 750 active users: ₹15,000/year.
- Additional 1,000 active users without additional student capacity: ₹6,000/year.
- Additional branch on Professional/Enterprise: ₹20,000/year per branch.

Rules:
- Essentials and Growth remain single-branch. Multi-branch clients must upgrade to Professional or Enterprise.
- White-label branding and custom domains remain Enterprise-only in the standard catalogue.
- Transport and Hostel remain Enterprise-only in the standard catalogue.
- Capacity extensions do not grant higher-tier feature entitlements.

## 7. Discount guardrails

The annual price already contains the standard annual-payment discount.

Commercial staff must not:
- discount the monthly list price;
- stack an additional annual discount automatically;
- offer a lower plan while manually enabling higher-tier modules;
- disable plan enforcement to close a deal;
- promise unlimited users/students/branches on a standard plan.

Any exceptional discount, strategic client price, multi-year deal or group contract must be approved as a written custom quote and represented in the SaaS platform with a dedicated plan or documented commercial override that preserves entitlement and limit enforcement.

## 8. Billing rules

- Monthly subscriptions: billed monthly in advance.
- Annual subscriptions: billed annually in advance.
- Subscription activation follows verified payment confirmation.
- Cancellation occurs at the paid period end when cancellation-at-period-end is selected.
- Past-due accounts retain only the existing billing-recovery access defined by the application.
- Prices are exclusive of applicable GST and third-party usage charges.
- Domestic standard-plan tax configuration is 18% GST.
- Export/SEZ/non-domestic invoicing requires finance review before using the standard tax configuration.

## 9. Public positioning

Use the following concise positioning in proposals:

- **Essentials** — Core ERP for small institutions.
- **Growth** — ERP + LMS + CRM + communication + examinations.
- **Professional** — Complete institutional operations with HR, finance, analytics and multi-branch.
- **Enterprise** — Full suite with white-label, custom domain, transport/hostel and high capacity.

Do not advertise every internal module independently. Sell outcomes: administration automation, academic operations, parent/student engagement, admissions conversion, fee control, institutional reporting and multi-campus governance.

## 10. Sales qualification

Recommend the smallest plan that satisfies both current requirements and near-term scale.

Use these default qualification anchors:
- Up to 300 active students, core ERP only -> Essentials.
- Up to 800 active students, single campus, LMS/CRM/exams needed -> Growth.
- Multi-branch, finance/HR/library/inventory/analytics -> Professional.
- White-label/custom domain, hostel/transport, large school group or high capacity -> Enterprise.

Do not recommend a lower tier if a required module is not entitled, even if the client's student count would otherwise fit.

## 11. Commercial review cadence

Review public list prices after:
- the first 10 paying external clients;
- the first 90 days of real implementation/support cost data; or
- a material infrastructure/payment-provider cost change.

Price changes apply to new quotations unless an existing signed contract says otherwise.
