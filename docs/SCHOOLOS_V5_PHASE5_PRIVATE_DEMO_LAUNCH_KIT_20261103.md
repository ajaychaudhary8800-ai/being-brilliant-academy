## 3. Private demonstration script — 25–35 minute agenda
**Preflight:** presenter confirms isolated demo fixture, nonbillable scenario, service healthy, correct sample tenant and mode, screen sharing clear of secrets/PII, and static disclaimer “Preview / not production service.” Do not invite real students/parents to a demo instance. Presenter must not imply V5 staging is a supported live service.
- **0–4 min:** institution pain points; ask school vs coaching, branch count, student count, existing software and must-have modules.
- **4–9 min:** tenant dashboard and roles; show permitted admin account and a rejected cross-tenant direct request through a controlled prepared evidence clip rather than probing customer records.
- **9–15 min:** admit synthetic learner, select section or batch, assign teacher, build timetable and record sample attendance.
- **15–21 min:** homework/exam workflow; student view and parent visibility only when synthetic account and module are verified.
- **21–26 min:** optional native classroom/recording demonstration **only** on pre-reviewed synthetic environment; otherwise present a clearly labeled prerecorded synthetic QA result. Do not claim AI provider/live media availability without observed valid session.
- **26–30 min:** product boundaries, plan/module/capacity subject to review, legal/data handling and staged implementation requirements.
- **30–35 min:** capture *consented* business-only requirements, decision authority and next step. Do not create approved customer quote, real invoice, subscription, payment or promise a pilot.

**Demo failure fallback:** if staging/security incident is unresolved, use approved static screenshots/sample workflow descriptions only, label evidence historical and never attempt credential workarounds. Record demo date, presenter, source SHA, fixtures, client consent, material questions and blocked features. Do not count a private synthetic demo as UAT or release Gate 7.

## 6. FAQ and objection handling
**“Is it live?”** V5 remains in controlled development/staging; commercial production release is not approved.  
**“Can you migrate student records?”** Only after a signed scoped agreement, authorized data processing and validated trial counts; no real records in private demo.  
**“Does it support schools and coaching?”** Both modes have source and synthetic QA coverage; real institutional acceptance depends on the selected module/release.  
**“Can a teacher work across branches?”** Coaching QA has previously tested authorized cross-branch allocation; school-mode restrictions differ. Verify release-specific permissions.  
**“Is AI grading included?”** No inclusion, accuracy or provider service should be promised before benchmark, consent and approved commercial/technical scope.  
**“Is video unlimited?”** No. Provider limits, tariffs and paid controls remain under review and paid flags OFF.  
**“How much?”** Quote V5 reference annual tiers only as explicitly **indicative ex tax**, verify current approved quote and entitlements. Enterprise custom.  
**“Is the 60-day pilot ₹14,999 available?”** Only a planning reference; no approved supported paid pilot checkout or offer.  
**“What uptime and recovery do you guarantee?”** Only a signed supported SLA applies; actual V5 monitoring, signed rollback and measured recovery remain acceptance items.  
**“Is your security independently certified?”** Do not claim certification. Owner-led AI-assisted internal risk-review option is documented, and outstanding incidents must be accepted before production.  
**“Can I subscribe now?”** No; sales materials are for a private demo and requirements qualification until written production/commercial GO.  
**“Will existing data be deleted at cancellation?”** Follow executed Order Form/DPA and approved exit/retention procedure; never invent immediate deletion.

**Weekly metrics (definitions only):** qualified leads / total opted-in leads, demo scheduled rate, demo attended rate, next-step acceptance, proposal acceptance, lost reason (unsupported capability, budget, integration, security/compliance, timing, no authority); no fabricated conversion percentages or customer names.
